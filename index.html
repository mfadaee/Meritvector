Can we tell real contribution from easy projects and noise?

MeritVector is evidence-based workforce intelligence for project management offices. It adjusts delivery outcomes for the conditions people worked under, shows the uncertainty, and says "insufficient evidence" when the data cannot support a conclusion. It never ranks people.
