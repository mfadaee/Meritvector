# Commercial strategy (hypotheses)

**Status:** hypotheses only. MeritVector has no customers, signed pilots, interviews or revenue. Nothing below
should be read as validated demand. Each claim states how it would be tested.

## Positioning

**Not a productivity tracker.** Trackers count activity. MeritVector:

- adjusts outcomes for the conditions work is done under
- shows uncertainty
- refuses to rank people
- will not turn activity into credit

The flagship study and the public-data validation are the evidence for this difference. They are also the main sales
asset.

| Alternative a buyer may use | How MeritVector differs (claim to test) |
|---|---|
| PPM tools' dashboards (RAG, on-time %) | Adjusts for complexity, dependencies and staffing, and explains why. |
| BI built in-house on the PPM export | Validated method, abstention, fairness audit and governance included. |
| Workforce-analytics or monitoring tools | No covert monitoring, no activity credit, no rankings; designed for regulated contexts. |
| Consultancy delivery reviews | Continuous and reproducible, rather than a one-off. |

## Go-to-market sequence (proposal)

1. **Discovery.** 15–25 conversations across the personas in [CUSTOMER_PERSONAS](CUSTOMER_PERSONAS.md).
   - **Exit criterion:** at least 3 organisations confirm the problem and have a budget holder willing to pilot.
2. **Design-partner pilots.** 2–3 pilots under [MVP_SCOPE](MVP_SCOPE.md).
   - **Exit criterion:** success criteria S1–S3 and S8 met in at least one pilot.
3. **Productise.** Production connectors for the PPM tools the pilots used, SSO, and security review.
4. **Expand.** Additional job families, each with its own job analysis and validation. Research modules become paid add-ons.

## Packaging hypotheses

| Module | Contents | Hypothesis to test |
|---|---|---|
| PMO Intelligence (core) | Portfolio, risk, bottlenecks, early warning, context analytics | Delivery leaders value context-adjusted portfolio insight without person-level views |
| Coordinator | Meeting extraction, tasks, reports, retrieval | Saves measurable coordination time (pilot criterion S6) |
| Evidence-based reviews | Person-level context-adjusted evidence, narratives, contribution accounts, review workflow | Organisations will adopt it only with strong governance; demand is uncertain and legally constrained |
| Research and audit | Research Laboratory, fairness audit, public benchmarks | People-analytics teams value auditability |

## Regulatory position

Employment-related AI is high-risk under the EU AI Act. Conformity obligations would apply before any EU deployment
of person-level features. The aggregated PMO modules may fall outside that scope; this needs legal review.

Works-council agreements and data-protection impact assessments are expected in many jurisdictions. See
[PRIVACY_AND_GOVERNANCE](../governance/PRIVACY_AND_GOVERNANCE.md).

## What we will not do

- sell ranking, promotion-readiness or dismissal outputs
- sell covert monitoring
- present synthetic-data results as customer outcomes
- quote revenue or market-size figures that have not been sourced and checked
