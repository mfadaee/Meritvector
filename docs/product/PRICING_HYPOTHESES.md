# Pricing hypotheses

**Every number on this page is a placeholder hypothesis for testing.** None comes from market research or
competitor price lists, and none has been validated with buyers.

## Candidate value metrics

| Metric | For | Against |
|---|---|---|
| Per active project under analysis | Tracks PMO value; does not tax headcount | Project counts vary in size and meaning |
| Per user seat | Familiar; simple | Discourages broad access (e.g. employees viewing their own records) |
| Platform fee per PMO plus module add-ons | Predictable; matches module packaging | Harder to scale with value |

**Working hypothesis:** a platform fee per PMO plus module add-ons. Employee self-service access is never priced
per seat, because charging for an employee's access to their own records would discourage transparency.

## Questions to answer before setting prices

1. What do target buyers currently spend on PPM analytics, delivery consulting and people analytics? *(Source:
   discovery interviews; do not estimate.)*
2. What is a pilot's measured value: time saved (S6), decisions supported (S4) and avoided slippage, expressed as a
   range?
3. Which budget pays: PMO, HR or transformation? Does that change the willingness to pay for each module?
4. Is the person-level evidence module viable at all once governance costs are included?

## Test design

- **Pilots:** design-partner pilots priced at a nominal or discounted fee. Record the sponsor's stated willingness to
  pay at week 12 (criterion S8), with reasons.
- **Discovery interviews:** use a structured method, such as van Westendorp price sensitivity or Gabor–Granger
  questions, once the value is concrete. Report results with sample sizes.
- **Revision:** revisit pricing only after at least 3 pilots or 20 qualified conversations, whichever comes first.

## Explicit non-claims

There is no revenue forecast, no market-size estimate and no competitor price comparison in this repository. Any
future figure must cite its source.
